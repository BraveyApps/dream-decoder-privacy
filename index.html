Privacy Policy for Dream Decoder AI

Effective Date: October 2, 2026

Dream Decoder AI ("Dream Decoder AI", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, how it may be stored or shared with service providers, and the choices available to you when you use the Dream Decoder AI mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the app, Dream Decoder AI may collect or process the following categories of information:

Account Information

If you create an account, we may process information associated with your account, including your email address, user account identifier, and profile information such as your display name.

Dream Information

When you use the dream interpretation features, you may provide dream descriptions or other text relating to your dreams. Dream Decoder AI processes this information to provide AI-generated dream interpretations and related features.

If you choose to save a dream to your journal, the dream description, title, AI-generated analysis, favorite status, and related journal information may be stored so that you can access your dream history and insights later.

Usage and App Data

The app may store information associated with your use of its features, such as dream interpretation usage limits, rewarded-ad usage, streak information, notification preferences, and app settings.

Voice Dictation

Dream Decoder AI provides an optional voice-dictation feature that allows you to speak your dream instead of typing it. When you use this feature, the device or operating-system speech recognition service may process your speech in order to produce text. Dream Decoder AI uses the resulting text as your dream input.

You are not required to use the voice-dictation feature and may enter your dream by typing instead.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by Dream Decoder AI may be used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Provide dream interpretation and related AI features.
  • Save and display your dream journal.
  • Provide dream insights and history.
  • Maintain usage limits and rewarded-ad benefits.
  • Provide subscription and premium features.
  • Remember app settings and preferences.
  • Maintain, secure, and operate the application.
  • Respond to support requests and resolve technical problems.

3. AI Dream Interpretation

Dream Decoder AI uses an AI-powered service to analyze information that you submit for dream interpretation.

Your submitted dream text may therefore be transmitted through our backend infrastructure to provide the requested AI interpretation and related conversational features.

Dream interpretations are provided for informational and entertainment purposes and should not be treated as medical, psychological, legal, financial, or other professional advice.

4. Supabase

Dream Decoder AI uses Supabase for authentication, database storage, and backend services.

Supabase may process information required to authenticate users and provide application functionality, including account information, profile information, saved dreams, AI analyses, and related application data.

Information stored through Supabase is used to provide features such as account access, dream journals, saved analyses, favorites, and usage-related features.

5. Advertising

Dream Decoder AI may display rewarded advertisements using Google AdMob.

Advertising providers may collect or process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location information, and information about ad interactions, subject to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

Advertising services may use this information to provide, measure, and improve advertising.

6. Subscriptions and Payments

Dream Decoder AI offers optional premium subscription features. Subscription management is supported through RevenueCat and the applicable app-store billing system.

RevenueCat may process information associated with purchases and subscriptions, such as subscription status, product information, transaction information, and an application user identifier, in order to provide subscription functionality, restore purchases, and determine access to premium features.

Payment card or other payment information used to complete an app-store purchase is handled by the applicable app store and its payment systems. Dream Decoder AI does not directly process your payment card details for app-store purchases.

7. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared with or processed by service providers that help us operate the application, including providers involved in:

  • Cloud hosting and database services.
  • User authentication.
  • AI processing.
  • Advertising.
  • Subscription management.
  • Application infrastructure and security.

These providers receive information only as reasonably necessary to provide their services or as otherwise permitted by applicable law.

8. Data Retention

We retain account and application information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the services, maintain your account, provide your saved dream journal, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

You may delete saved dreams through the features provided within the app. Where the app provides functionality to clear your dream journal, using that functionality removes the applicable saved dream records from the application's database, subject to backups, legal requirements, and legitimate operational requirements.

9. Account and Data Deletion

If you would like to request deletion of your account or personal information, please contact us using the contact information provided below.

We may need to verify the request before completing it. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or where necessary for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, accounting, or legal purposes.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information processed through Dream Decoder AI.

However, no method of electronic transmission, storage, or processing can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Children's Privacy

Dream Decoder AI is not specifically designed or directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the app without appropriate authorization, please contact us so that appropriate action can be considered.

12. Third-Party Services

Dream Decoder AI uses third-party services to provide certain functionality. These services may have their own privacy policies governing information they process.

These services include:

  • Supabase – authentication, database, and backend services.
  • Google AdMob – advertising services.

13. Your Choices

Depending on the features available on your device and in the app, you may be able to:

  • Choose whether to create or use an account.
  • Choose whether to use voice dictation.
  • Choose whether to save dreams to your journal.
  • Delete saved dreams through available app functionality.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated personal information.
  • Manage app permissions through your device settings.
  • Manage advertising and privacy choices through applicable device or advertising settings.
